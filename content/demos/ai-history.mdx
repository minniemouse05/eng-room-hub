# The History of AI

AI might seem brand new, but the dream of thinking machines is actually decades old. Let's journey through time to see how we got here.

---

## The Timeline

![Timeline of AI history from 1950s to 2020s](/images/ai-timeline.svg)

---

## <span style={{color: '#f472b6'}}>1950s</span> — The Birth of AI

> <span style={{color: '#f472b6'}}>**Era:** Founding & Optimism</span>

In 1956, a group of scientists gathered at Dartmouth College with a bold question: *Can machines think?*

This workshop, organized by **John McCarthy**, officially coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" and launched an entirely new field of science.

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1950 | Alan Turing proposes the "Turing Test" |
| 1956 | Dartmouth Conference — AI is born |
| 1957 | First neural network (Perceptron) created |

> **The vibe:** Researchers were *wildly* optimistic. Many predicted human-level AI within 20 years. Spoiler: they were wrong.

---

## <span style={{color: '#c084fc'}}>1960s-70s</span> — Early Wins, Then Winter

> <span style={{color: '#c084fc'}}>**Era:** Excitement → Disappointment ❄️</span>

The 60s brought exciting demos — programs that could solve algebra, prove theorems, and even chat (sort of). **ELIZA** (1966) was an early chatbot that fooled some people into thinking it understood them.

**But then reality hit.**

Computers were too slow. Memory was too limited. The grand visions couldn't be achieved with bicycle parts.

| What Happened | Result |
|---------------|--------|
| Overpromised results | Funding dried up |
| Limited computing power | Progress stalled |
| 1974-1980 | <span style={{color: '#c084fc'}}>**First "AI Winter"**</span> |

> **Plain English:** Imagine promising to build a rocket ship but only having bicycle parts. That was early AI — big dreams, tiny tools.

---

## <span style={{color: '#818cf8'}}>1980s</span> — Expert Systems Rise (Then Fall)

> <span style={{color: '#818cf8'}}>**Era:** Practical Applications</span>

Researchers got smarter. Instead of trying to create *general* intelligence, they built **Expert Systems** — programs that mimicked human experts in *specific* areas.

**Real-world uses:**
- Banks used them to approve loans
- Doctors used them to diagnose diseases
- Companies paid millions for these systems

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1980 | Expert systems go commercial |
| 1986 | Neural network training improves |
| Late 80s | <span style={{color: '#818cf8'}}>**Second AI Winter**</span> |

> **The lesson:** AI works best on *specific* problems, not "do everything" dreams. But expert systems were expensive and brittle — when conditions changed, they broke.

---

## <span style={{color: '#38bdf8'}}>1990s</span> — Machine Learning Changes Everything

> <span style={{color: '#38bdf8'}}>**Era:** Learning from Data ♟️</span>

A fundamental shift happened. Instead of programming explicit rules, researchers taught computers to **learn from data**.

**The breakthrough moment:** In 1997, IBM's **Deep Blue** defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov. A machine beat the best human at chess.

| Old Approach | New Approach |
|--------------|--------------|
| Write every rule by hand | Let the computer find patterns |
| Rigid, breaks easily | Flexible, improves with data |
| "If X, then Y" | "Learn from examples" |

> **Think of it this way:** Instead of writing a recipe for every dish, machine learning lets the computer learn to cook by tasting thousands of meals.

---

## <span style={{color: '#22d3ee'}}>2000s</span> — The Ingredients Come Together

> <span style={{color: '#22d3ee'}}>**Era:** Data + Power + Algorithms</span>

Three things finally aligned:

| Ingredient | What Changed |
|------------|--------------|
| **Big Data** | The internet generated massive training data |
| **Computing Power** | Processors got fast enough to crunch it |
| **Better Algorithms** | Deep learning techniques emerged |

**Key moments:**
- **2006:** Geoffrey Hinton's "deep learning" breakthrough
- **2009:** Google uses AI to improve search
- **Late 2000s:** Smartphones bring AI to your pocket

> **The shift:** AI moved from research labs to products you actually use.

---

## <span style={{color: '#34d399'}}>2010s</span> — The Deep Learning Revolution

> <span style={{color: '#34d399'}}>**Era:** AI Gets *Really* Good 🧠</span>

Deep learning — using layers of artificial "neurons" — produced dramatic improvements almost overnight.

| Year | Breakthrough |
|------|--------------|
| 2011 | IBM Watson wins Jeopardy! |
| 2012 | Image recognition accuracy jumps |
| 2014 | Alexa & Siri become household names |
| 2016 | AlphaGo beats world Go champion |
| 2017 | Transformer architecture invented |

**Why Go mattered:** Chess has roughly 35 possible moves per turn. Go has ~250. Experts said AI couldn't handle it. They were wrong.

> **The result:** AI went from "cool demo" to "essential technology." Your phone's camera, spam filter, and Netflix recommendations all run on AI from this era.

---

## <span style={{color: '#fbbf24'}}>2020s</span> — AI for Everyone

> <span style={{color: '#fbbf24'}}>**Era:** Mainstream & Accessible 🚀</span>

Large language models showed AI could understand and generate human language with unprecedented skill. Suddenly, *anyone* could use AI — not just tech companies.

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 2020 | GPT-3 shows remarkable language abilities |
| 2022 | DALL-E & Midjourney create images from text |
| 2022-23 | ChatGPT reaches 100M users in 2 months |
| 2023+ | AI assistants integrate into everyday tools |

> **Where we are now:** You can use AI to write, code, create art, answer questions, and more. We're living through one of the fastest technology shifts in history.

---

## What History Teaches Us

| Lesson | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Progress isn't linear** | AI had multiple "winters" — don't believe every hype cycle |
| **Specific beats general** | Focused AI succeeds; "do everything" AI fails |
| **Infrastructure matters** | Many techniques waited decades for fast enough computers |
| **Expectations should be realistic** | We *still* don't have human-level AI after 70 years |

---

## Key Takeaways

> - <span style={{color: '#f472b6'}}>**1956:**</span> AI was born at the Dartmouth Conference
> - <span style={{color: '#c084fc'}}>**1970s & 80s:**</span> Two "AI Winters" when funding and interest collapsed
> - <span style={{color: '#38bdf8'}}>**1990s:**</span> Machine learning replaced hand-coded rules
> - <span style={{color: '#34d399'}}>**2010s:**</span> Deep learning made AI actually *work* at scale
> - <span style={{color: '#fbbf24'}}>**2020s:**</span> AI became accessible to everyone, not just researchers
